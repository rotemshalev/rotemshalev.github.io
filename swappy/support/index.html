Swappy Support

Swappy helps pilates and fitness instructors and studios in Israel find cover for classes and stay connected with their professional community.

Contact

Email: swappy.platform@gmail.com

We usually reply within two business days. Please include the email address of your Swappy account, your phone model, and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

I didn't get my verification code. Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then ask for a new code on the same screen. Codes expire after a short time.

How do I report a person or a post? Tap the ⋯ menu on the post, comment, or profile and choose Report. You can also block the person from the same menu. Reports are reviewed by the Swappy team.

How do I change the app language? Open the menu (☰), then Language. Swappy supports Hebrew and English.

How do I delete my account? See Account deletion.
