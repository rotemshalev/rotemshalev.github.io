Swappy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19

Last updated: 2026-10-19

1. Who controls your information

  • Controller: Rotem Shalev, an individual operating Swappy from Israel
  • Contact for privacy matters: swappy.platform@gmail.com
  • Data protection officer or EU representative: not appointed. Swappy is operated from Israel and does not currently target users in the European Union.

2. Scope

Swappy helps fitness studios and instructors discover each other, coordinate open-shift coverage, maintain professional profiles, connect, and share community content. This policy covers the Swappy web application, mobile application, APIs, support interactions, and optional Arbox integration.

Swappy is intended only for adults acting professionally. Users must be at least 18 years old. Swappy does not knowingly permit or collect personal information from anyone under 18.

3. Information Swappy processes

Account and profile information

  • Name, email address, password hash, account role, and verification status
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google: the account identifier and the email address that Apple or Google shares with Swappy
  • Profile photos, studio logos and covers
  • Phone number, city, studio address, biography, qualifications, specialties, and social links
  • Active and past professional relationships between studios and instructors

Marketplace and operational information

  • Open shifts, instructor shift-posting requests, coverage requests, approvals, cancellations, and work-connection requests
  • Schedule information entered in Swappy or read from an optional studio-authorized Arbox connection. An eligible instructor may receive only their own assigned upcoming classes for class selection.
  • Arbox configuration state and encrypted API credentials for studio owners who configure it

Community information

  • Posts, comments, reactions, reviews, connections, follows, reports, blocks, and moderation records

Location and device information

  • Precise location only when a user chooses the Near me feature
  • Coarse profile or studio location supplied by the user
  • Exact venue address and coordinates attached to an active instructor-published swap
  • IP-derived request metadata, security events, and limited operational request/error logs
  • Crash and error reports from the app and the server: the error, the screen or request route, and device and app version details. They exclude IP addresses, email addresses, screenshots and the content of what you post
  • Camera or photo-library content only when the user chooses to provide an image
  • A push notification address for each phone where the user turns on notifications, linked to the signed-in session (removed from use when the user signs out), and the user's notification choices

Support and privacy requests

  • Support messages, abuse reports, privacy requests, verification information, and related responses

Swappy does not sell personal information, use advertising SDKs, or track users across third-party apps or websites in the current release.

5. What is public and what is restricted

Swappy's content is available only to signed-in members; visitors who are not signed in see only the sign-in and policy screens. Within Swappy, profiles may show professional identity, photos, city, biography, qualifications, specialties, and intentionally published workplace/history summaries. They do not expose private account identifiers, phone or email unless authorized, exact instructor coordinates, private notes, encrypted credentials, or future personal schedules.

Members may see active swap details, including the class time, venue address, exact map point, and the posting instructor's public professional summary. Applicant data, contact details, and private request messages are shown only to the people involved. Pending, declined, withdrawn, and cancelled coverage requests remain private to their authorized participants.

Operational contact details may be shown to approved connections or counterparties when necessary to complete a requested marketplace action. Studios publish their address and map location to members by default, since they are businesses members need to find, and may switch this off; business phone and email are shown only if the studio chooses. A venue address and exact coordinates attached to an active swap are visible to members while that swap is discoverable.

  • Google (Gmail) to send verification and account emails

  • Apple, Google and Expo services needed to build and distribute the mobile applications
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim for studio address geocoding
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), with data stored in its EU region (Frankfurt), for crash and error reports
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver phone notifications: the notification's title and text and the phone's push address
  • Arbox when a studio owner configures and uses the optional integration
  • Professional advisers, regulators, courts, or law enforcement where legally required
  • A successor in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale, subject to applicable notice and law

Swappy does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

7. International transfers

Swappy's servers and database are hosted in Germany (European Union). Some service providers listed above are based in the United States and may process limited information there, under their own contractual and security commitments. Swappy is operated from Israel.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to:

  • Access and obtain a portable copy of personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete an account and associated content
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent
  • Opt out of sale or sharing, if Swappy ever introduces activity that qualifies
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You can download your data, correct your profile, submit privacy requests, and delete your account in the app (Menu, Privacy and support). You can also email swappy.platform@gmail.com. More details: https://rotemshalev.github.io/swappy/privacy-choices/

Swappy verifies requests before disclosing or deleting information. Users will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights.

10. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Menu, Privacy and support, Delete account). Deletion removes the account and associated personal information and content, except limited records that must be kept for a defined legal or safety reason (such as an open abuse report), as described in the retention table above. Instructions: https://rotemshalev.github.io/swappy/account-deletion/

11. Security

Swappy uses HTTPS, password hashing, revocable sessions, least-privilege authorization, encrypted Arbox credentials, image decoding and re-encoding, secret redaction, dependency scanning, and security monitoring. No system is perfectly secure. Please report security issues privately to swappy.platform@gmail.com, not publicly.

12. Changes

When this policy changes materially, Swappy updates the effective date and asks you to review and accept the new version in the app. Previous versions and acceptance records are kept.
