Delete your Swappy account

You can delete your Swappy account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the menu (☰) at the top of the screen.
  2. Open Privacy and support.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email swappy.platform@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, photos, posts, comments, reactions, connections, follows, swaps, and requests are deleted.

What may be kept

  • Limited records needed for an open safety report or a legal obligation, for up to 24 months
  • Backups, from which deleted information disappears as they expire, within 30 days

See the Privacy Policy for details.
