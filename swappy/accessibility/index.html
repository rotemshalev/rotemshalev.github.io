Swappy Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 2026-10-19

Swappy aims to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA for the web application and to provide equivalent accessible operation in the iOS and Android applications using VoiceOver, TalkBack, Switch Control, Dynamic Type, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and sufficient touch targets.

Measures

  • Accessibility requirements are included in design, development, review, and release gates.
  • Shared controls expose names, roles, states, errors, focus indicators, and status announcements.
  • Maps provide accessible result-list alternatives.
  • Motion respects user preferences.
  • Automated checks are supplemented by keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, target-size, and maximum-text-size testing.

Current status

Swappy does not yet claim full WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. Work still in progress:

  • An independent contrast and touch-target audit
  • Full VoiceOver and Switch Control testing on a physical iPhone, and TalkBack on an Android phone
  • Layouts at the largest text sizes
  • Some map, calendar, and modal journeys
