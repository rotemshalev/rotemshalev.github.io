Your privacy choices

You control your information in Swappy.

In the app (Menu, then Privacy and support)

  • Privacy requests and history: ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or other privacy requests, and follow their status
  • Delete account: permanently delete your account and data
  • Edit your profile: correct or remove profile details, photos, and contact information
  • Notifications: choose which notifications you get (Menu, then Notifications)
  • Studio location: studios can choose whether their address and map location are shown (Edit profile)

Swappy does not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

By email

Write to swappy.platform@gmail.com from your account's email address. We verify requests before sharing or deleting information.

See the Privacy Policy for your rights and how long information is kept.
