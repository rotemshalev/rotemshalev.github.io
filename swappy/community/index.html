Swappy Community Guidelines

Effective date: 2026-10-19

Swappy is a professional community. These rules apply to profiles, posts, comments, reviews, images, connections, shift listings, messages submitted with requests, and offline conduct coordinated through the service.

Be authentic

  • Use an identity and professional information you are authorized to present.
  • Do not impersonate people or studios.
  • Do not fabricate qualifications, workplaces, shifts, reviews, availability, or endorsements.

Protect safety and privacy

  • Do not threaten, stalk, harass, exploit, intimidate, or encourage violence.
  • Do not publish private contact, location, schedule, financial, health, or identity information without authorization.
  • Do not use Swappy to monitor another person's movements or future schedule.
  • For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Swappy is not an emergency service.

Respect people

  • Do not post hateful, discriminatory, sexually exploitative, degrading, or bullying content.
  • Do not target people based on protected characteristics.
  • Keep reviews factual, relevant, and based on genuine completed interactions.

Follow the law and professional rules

  • Do not facilitate fraud, unsafe work, illegal employment, prohibited discrimination, or unlicensed services.
  • Respect copyright, trademarks, privacy, publicity, and other rights.
  • Do not upload malware, deceptive links, spam, or content that interferes with the service.

Report and block

Use the in-app Report action for content or accounts that violate these rules or may be illegal. Use Block to stop non-operational interaction with an abusive user. Reports involving immediate threats, child safety, exploitation, or serious illegal conduct receive priority.

Reports can cover posts, comments, studio reviews, instructor profiles, studio profiles, shifts, and account behavior. The server resolves the owner and captures a sanitized evidence snapshot. Do not add unnecessary personal information to the optional details field.

Blocking hides both people from ordinary discovery and interaction, closes pending unaccepted social, work, follow, and swap requests, and prevents new requests. It does not erase accepted or current operational obligations. A studio cannot suppress a review merely by blocking its author.

Published support, Community Guidelines, and abuse-contact URLs are configured through SUPPORT_URL, COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES_URL, and ABUSE_CONTACT_URL. Production release remains blocked until all three are final public HTTPS URLs.

Moderation

Swappy may preserve a bounded evidence snapshot, hide or remove content, suspend or restore accounts, or contact authorities where required. Administrator actions require a reason and timestamp. Reviews and other UGC must be moderated under content-neutral rules. Negative sentiment or a studio's request alone is never a valid removal reason.

Reports are reviewed by the Swappy team, usually within 72 hours; reports about immediate safety are handled first. To report something, use Report in the app, or email swappy.platform@gmail.com. In an emergency, contact the local emergency services first.

Future legal or scale triggers

Swappy does not currently implement a public DSA notice form, trusted-flagger workflow, automated statements of reasons, in-app appeals, or transparency-report automation. Add those only if release territories, legal advice, moderation volume, or platform scale make them necessary.
